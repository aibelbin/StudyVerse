# Mini-Golf Implementation Plan

> Executed inline. One full review runs at the end (user preference: no per-step reviews).

**Goal:** implement `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-mini-golf-design.md`.

## Task 1: Shared course data and scene

- **New file `shared/golf.ts`:**
  - `HOLES`, pieces, `windmillAngle`, `scoreName`, `GOLF_BOX`.
- **Constants and protocol:** the golf messages, the `golfBoard` `ToyState` and the `bad_golf` error.
- **Scene:**
  - Paths: the extended strip path, the walkway and the spur.
  - Strip gaps: remove the two east gaps.
  - Tidy area: grass and rocks kept out of `GOLF_BOX`.
  - The scoreboard toy and the walker colliders.
- **Tests:** `shared/golf.test.ts` and scenes.

## Task 2: Server

- **Per-player golf state, plus `golfTee`, `golfShot`, `golfBall`, `golfHoled`, `golfPickUp` and `golfQuit`.**
- **The board as a saved toy state; KP and feed lines.**
- **Cleanup on disconnect, scene change and sitting down.**
- **Validation.**
- **Tests:** `world.golf.test.ts`.

## Task 3: Ball physics

- **New file `client/src/golf/sim.ts`:** `GolfSim` builds a Rapier world from a hole and handles putts, steps, rest, penalties and holing.
- **Pure helpers:** `aimFromDrag`, `cupCheck`, `RestDetector`.
- **Tests:** a short Node physics run.

## Task 4: Client

- **`client/src/golf/course.ts`:** course visuals, windmill, flags, scoreboard.
- **`client/src/golf/play.ts`:** drag input, aim line, power bar, HUD card, camera follow, streaming, other players' balls.
- **Hooks into `game.ts`:** tee prompts and E routing, messages, Q to leave, and the camera.
- **Minimap.**

## Task 5: Final checks

- Headless checks with real mouse drags and a bot.
- One review.
- Push.
- Ask before deploying.
